A tool for turning raw phone clips into scroll-ready reels

One set of clips.
Five different edits.

Pick a few videos from your Photos app. This app auto-cuts them into five reels with different pacing, order, transitions and music — so you can post the version that actually performs, or try more than one.

Choose your clips

Pick 2 or more videos. Longer clips work fine — each edit will pull the best-fit portion automatically.

Add music (optional)

Some edits use it, some don't — that's part of what makes the five reels different.
